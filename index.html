ASSISTIVE ROBOTICS / HCI2024–present

GainSight

Submitted to CHI

Assistive indoor navigation

GainSight explores how spatial audio can support indoor navigation for blind and low-vision users. A human-pushed walker combines 2D LiDAR and inertial sensing with map-based localization to turn route information into directional audio cues.

The interface pairs spatial sound through headphones with brief spoken prompts for turns and arrival, connecting guidance to the user’s position and heading.

My work spans ROS 2 system integration, mapping and localization, sensor fusion, and the connection between route planning and audio rendering, alongside user-centered evaluation.

Advised by Prof. Ming-Chun Huang

EMBEDDED AI / AGENTS2025–2026

IoT-SkillsBench

Evaluating AI agents on real embedded hardware

Code that compiles can still fail on a physical device. I co-developed a hardware-in-the-loop benchmark and reusable expert skills for AI agents working with Arduino, ESP-IDF, and Zephyr.

My work includes hardware skill modules and agent workflows that carry generated firmware through compilation, flashing, serial validation, and real-device testing.

Abstract

We introduce a skills-based framework for AI agents developing embedded and IoT systems, together with IoT-SkillsBench: 42 tasks across three embedded platforms and 23 peripherals. The benchmark compares no-skill, LLM-generated-skill, and human-expert-skill configurations across 378 evaluation instances, validating generated firmware through execution on real hardware.
